I finished my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at NYU in Spring 2026 and joined L2 Labs as a research scientist in Fall 2026. There I work on time series forecasting, anomaly detection, and a bit of generative AI.

My dissertation explores the connection between variational mechanics and multi-agent learning (which pretty much can be encapsulated into this algorithmic suite). Other than that, I mostly work at the intersection of stochastic control, RL, and game theory, and lately I’ve been really interested in deep hedging.

I’m currently reading Infinite Jest by DFW.
